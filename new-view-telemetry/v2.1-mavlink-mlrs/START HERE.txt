V2.1 MAVLINK AND MLRS NEW VIEW TELEMETRY  by NVFPV  (tester build)
Live satellite map and flight telemetry for EdgeTX colour radios
================================================================

FULL STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS: "INSTRUCTIONS - STEP BY STEP.txt" (start there).
mLRS / ArduPilot / MAVLink users: also read "MLRS AND MAVLINK SETUP.txt".
Pictures: "New View Telemetry - User Guide.pdf" and the "screenshots" folder.
When you have tested it, fill in "TESTER FEEDBACK FORM.txt" and send it back.
The video tutorial is in the "video" folder.

YOU NEED
  - An EdgeTX colour-screen radio on EdgeTX 2.11 or later (HelloRadioSky V15: EdgeTX 2.12 or later)
  - ExpressLRS or Crossfire with telemetry on, OR mLRS with the Tx module set to CRSF
  - A flight controller with a GPS: Betaflight or INAV over CRSF, or ArduPilot (MAVLink)
    or INAV through mLRS

QUICK INSTALL
  1. Copy the WIDGETS and IMAGES folders from "SD card" onto the root of the radio's SD card.
     Let them merge with the folders already there.
  2. Make map tiles for your flying area with a tool in "PC tools" and copy them into
     /IMAGES/NewView/sat_tiles/ on the SD card. This pack has no map tiles.
     See "PC tools\HOW TO MAKE MAP TILES.txt" or section 5 of the guide.
  3. On the radio, with the craft powered: Model > Telemetry > Discover new sensors.
  4. Add a screen with the 1x1 layout (App mode on for full screen), select the widget
     "New View Telemetry", then set it up in Widget settings:
       Zoom          a 3-position switch: up zooms in, down zooms out
       Min/Max zoom  the zoom levels you made tiles for (0 to 2 by default)
       Home reset    a switch that moves home to where the craft is
       Cursor        Arrow, Drone or Plane
       Limits        max altitude and distance, min cell voltage, battery %, link quality and satellites

Until the first GPS fix the widget shows the load screen with "NO TELEMETRY" or
"WAITING FOR GPS - SATS n". At the first fix it switches to the map.
