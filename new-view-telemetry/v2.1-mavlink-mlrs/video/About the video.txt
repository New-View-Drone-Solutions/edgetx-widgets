New View Telemetry - Video Tutorial.mp4 (1920x1080, about 4 minutes, captions on screen, with background music)

It shows each step: copying the files, making map tiles, finding the telemetry sensors,
adding the widget and its settings, then the load screen, flying, zoom, home reset,
warnings and GPS lost. Recorded in the EdgeTX simulator (HelloRadioSky V15) with simulated telemetry.

The music is an original track made for New View Telemetry, so it is free to share with the video.
